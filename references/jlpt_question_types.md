# JLPT-style Question Types for Lyric-Based Lessons

Use the lyrics as source material, but design questions that train transferable written JLPT skills for the user's target level.

## 文字・語彙

- 汉字读法: choose the reading of a kanji word from the lyric context.
- 表记/词形: choose the correct kanji/kana form when useful.
- 前后关系: choose a word that fits a short context sentence.
- 近义替换: choose the closest expression in Japanese or Chinese.
- 用法: choose the sentence where a target word is used naturally.

Level notes:

- N5/N4: focus on common readings, basic meanings, and simple usage.
- N3: add contextual word choice, compound verbs, and common abstract nouns.
- N2/N1: add nuance, written expressions, abstract vocabulary, and figurative use.

## 文法

- 语法形式判断: choose the correct particle, ending, or grammar pattern.
- 句子组织: reorder fragments into a grammatical sentence.
- 文章语法: choose a connector or expression that fits a short passage.
- 语感辨析: compare similar patterns, but only when the target level justifies it.

Example targets by level:

- N5: `は/が/を/に`, basic adjective and verb forms, `です/ます`.
- N4: `Vている`, `Vてくる`, `Vても`, conditionals, `より`.
- N3: `Vだす`, `Vきる`, `ように`, `わけではない`, common discourse connectors.
- N2: `なんかじゃない`, `なくとも`, `にすぎない`, `わけだ`, written-style connectors.
- N1: literary usage, subtle modality, advanced set phrases, dense sentence structure.

## 読解

- 内容理解: short passage about the lyric's theme, then ask the main point.
- 信息检索: a schedule, study plan, vocabulary card, or mini table related to the lesson.
- 综合理解: compare two short interpretations and ask what they share or differ on.

Do not ask students to recall exact lyric lines. Ask them to infer theme, relationship, mood, or function of an expression.

## Do Not Use

- Listening simulation or teacher-read listening scripts.
- Full lyric blank filling.
- Whole-line memorization.
- Questions that require song trivia.
- Overly literary interpretation with no language-learning target.
